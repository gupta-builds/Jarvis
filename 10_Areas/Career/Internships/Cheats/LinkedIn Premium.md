# LinkedIn Search Research
Use this to run a manual LinkedIn search for a specific internship or a person connected to it.
## Find a role
==Write the role and company into the search before applying filters.==
Try `fullstack intern` with a target company and location. Use the posting to verify the role title, employer and application page before recording it.
## Find a contact
==Search for a role the person is likely to list on their profile.==
Start with the company and `university recruiter` or `early careers`. Check the person’s employer and responsibilities before adding them to a Contact note. Write outreach with [[10_Areas/Career/Internships/Contacts/Mimic|Mimic]].
