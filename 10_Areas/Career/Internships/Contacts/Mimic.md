# Mimic
Use this public reference to draft a message for a real recipient. Replace the brackets with facts you can check.
## First contact
==Give the recipient a reason to recognize their team in the note.==
Hi [Name], I saw the [Role] opening and wanted to ask about [specific part of the team’s work]. Would you be open to connecting?
## Application follow-up
==Check the Tracker at seven and fourteen days and log each sent message in its Timeline.==
Hi [Name], I applied for [Role] on [date]. I’m following up to ask about the timeline and would be happy to send any details that would help.
## Referral request
==Ask after you have a relationship with the person.==
Hi [Name], I’m pursuing [Role] and thought of our conversation about [specific topic]. Would you be comfortable pointing me to the right recruiter or referring me?
## After the interview
==Name one point from the conversation.==
Thanks for explaining [specific detail] during our interview. It helped me understand how the team approaches [problem]. I appreciated the conversation.
