Are you interested in an internship that provides you an opportunity to turn your related coursework into real-world business experiences? Do you thrive on fostering collaboration with others and working with a team?
The Federal Home Loan Bank of Indianapolis is seeking well-rounded and motivated students to join our team. The student filling this position will handle a wide range of important duties. We seek individuals who thrive in challenging and diverse environments, are motivated goal achievers, and have a willingness to learn.
**Position Summary:**
The Data Platform Engineer Intern will join the Data Platform Engineering team to gain hands-on experience supporting the Bank’s enterprise data platforms and modern analytics technologies. This role offers exposure to Microsoft Fabric, Microsoft Azure SQL, SAP BusinessObjects, and other critical systems that enable data-driven decision-making. Interns will work under the guidance of senior engineers and collaborate on projects that improve platform reliability, performance, and automation while learning industry best practices.
**Core Responsibilities:**
- **Platform Support:** Assist with deployments across environments (Development → Test → Production) for database objects, SAP BusinessObjects, and Microsoft Fabric components (Power BI reports, pipelines, notebooks, dataflows).
- **Troubleshooting & Service Requests:** Help resolve user access issues, report and query troubleshooting, and data connectivity problems using ServiceNow and Azure DevOps.
- **Performance & Security:** Support monitoring activities and follow established security and compliance standards.
- **Automation & Infrastructure:** Collaborate on scripting and automation projects using Python, PowerShell, T-SQL, PL/SQL, and assist in developing Terraform modules for Microsoft Fabric.
- **Migration & Modernization:** Contribute to initiatives analytical and reporting solutions to Microsoft Fabric using Lakehouse/Data Warehouse architecture, notebooks, and pipelines.
- **Training & Enablement:** Assist in creating training materials (playbooks, videos, design standards) to upskill Bank staff on Microsoft Fabric and Power BI.
**Position Requirements:**
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Engineering, or related field.
- Familiarity with database fundamentals (tables, views, stored procedures) and basic SQL/T-SQL or PL/SQL.
- Exposure to Microsoft SQL Server, Microsoft Fabric, or similar platforms preferred.
- Basic understanding of scripting languages (Python, PowerShell) and version control tools.
- Interest in cloud technologies (Azure), infrastructure concepts (VMs, networking, storage), and Agile methodology.
- Strong problem-solving skills, eagerness to learn, and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
**Key Learning Outcomes:**
- Hands-on experience with enterprise data platforms and modern analytics tools.
- Practical exposure to deployment automation, infrastructure-as-code, and cloud-based data solutions.
- Opportunity to contribute to real-world projects that enhance data delivery speed and platform reliability.
