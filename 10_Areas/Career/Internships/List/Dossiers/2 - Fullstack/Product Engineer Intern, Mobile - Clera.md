### About the Role
Join a consumer mobile app team to maintain and improve a live product used by a growing audience. You will contribute across the development lifecycle, from refining screens to shipping releases.
### What You'll Do
- Fix bugs and crashes to keep the app reliable for users.
- Build and ship small features end to end, from design through release.
- Refine visual details and interactions so screens feel native.
- Maintain builds, releases, and Firebase services.
- Test the app on physical iOS and Android devices.
### What We're Looking For
- Zero to two years of experience, including current students.
- Experience shipping at least one mobile app that attracted real users; personal and side projects count.
- Working experience with Flutter and Firebase in an existing codebase.
- Ability to make changes independently, ship features end to end, and work without line-by-line supervision.
- Availability for 30 to 60 hours per week for at least two months.
- Experience building software with AI tools and the ability to explain the resulting code.
- A strong eye for visual and interaction details that help mobile screens feel native.
- Familiarity with Swift, Kotlin, or TypeScript is relevant to the team's stack.
### Compensation & Benefits
Compensation is $4,000 to $8,000 per month. No equity or visa sponsorship details were provided.

On-site or remote in the United States. San Francisco Bay Area is also listed as a location.
