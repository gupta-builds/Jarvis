# Intern - Software Engineer
Micron Technology — Boise, ID, USA
## Qualifications
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Data Science, or a related field; cannot graduate prior to September 2027.
Experience with Python, JavaScript, C#, or similar programming languages, familiarity with databases and SQL, knowledge of REST APIs and web services, and understanding of software development concepts and version control systems such as Git.
Interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning, or large language models is a plus.
## Role
Member of the Mask Technology Center (MTC) software engineering team, building AI-assisted tools supporting semiconductor manufacturing operations. Participate in the full development lifecycle: requirements gathering, solution design, coding, testing, deployment, and user feedback.

Micron's internship program runs May through August (Summer).
