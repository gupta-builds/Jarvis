SRAM Software Engineer Intern, BS - Summer 2027
Location: Burlington, VT | Marvell Technology
Marvell's semiconductor solutions are the essential building blocks of the data infrastructure that connects our world -- powering the data economy across cloud, 5G, enterprise, and AI infrastructure.
As a SRAM Software Engineer Intern, you'll work with the SRAM Compiler engineering team developing software tools that generate SRAM memory compilers used across Marvell's semiconductor product lines. You'll gain hands-on experience with EDA software development, scripting (Python/Perl/Tcl), and the physical-design flow that turns compiler output into silicon-ready memory blocks.
Requirements: Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field. Programming experience in Python, C/C++, or similar. Interest in EDA tooling, chip design automation, or systems software.
Marvell internships connect students to meaningful projects, experienced mentors, and technologies shaping the future of data infrastructure.
