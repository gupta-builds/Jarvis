# CPT Program
Curricular practical training is the work permission that used to make a summer internship simple for an F-1 student, and in 2026 it is the one most disrupted. This note holds how CPT works, what changed in August, what UMN's pages say as of September, and what is realistically left for a computer science major. Read it before planning any internship that starts before graduation.
## What CPT Is
==CPT is training the school treats as part of the degree, which the school's DSO authorizes on the I-20 for one employer and one period, with no USCIS application and no EAD.== The regulation limits it to "an integral part of an established curriculum" and defines it as "alternative work/study, internship, cooperative education or any other type of required internship or practicum that is offered by sponsoring employers through cooperative agreements with the school" (8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i)).
The mechanics follow from that. The student requests it from the DSO, may begin only after receiving an I-20 with the DSO's endorsement, and the DSO records in SEVIS whether it is full-time or part-time, the employer and location, and the start and end dates. Authorization is for a specific employer and period, so a new employer needs a new I-20 ([[SEVP and DSOs]]).
- *Eligibility:* one full academic year of full-time enrollment, with exceptions for graduate programs that require immediate training.
- *Against OPT:* CPT is school-authorized and tied to the curriculum, OPT is USCIS-authorized and need not be part of any course ([[OPT Program]]).
- *The cost that matters later:* one year or more of full-time CPT makes a student ineligible for post-completion OPT, but part-time CPT does not. ISSS adds that CPT time is generally not subtracted from the 12 months of OPT.
## Two Readings Of "Integral"
Until August 2026 the working reading was two pathways: training that earns academic credit qualifies even if it is elective, and non-credit training qualifies if it is required for the degree. Since August SEVP says only training required of every student in the curriculum qualifies. The reasons each side gives, the 1991 rulemaking history, and the pending challenge are in [[CPT Lawsuit]], and the exact words of the new reading are in [[CPT Broadcast Messages]].
The difference decides my case. A CS student taking an optional internship course for credit fit the old reading and fails the new one.
## Where CPT Stands On 2026-10-23
- The August 12 and 24 messages are in force. No court has stayed them.
- Plaintiffs including the AAU, of which the University of Minnesota is a member, are asking for a stay and injunction. The hearing is November 6 and the government's response is due October 28.
- Many schools have narrowed or paused CPT. The plaintiffs' filings name sixteen, and UMN, which is not among them, has also paused every application.
- The regulation itself is unchanged, and so is the CPT definition that the OPT fee proposal repeats. That proposal covers OPT only and says it "does not address CPT" ([[OPT Fee Proposal]]).
## UMN Snapshot
This is the state of UMN's pages in September 2026 and is queued for a rewrite.
1. **The pause**
	ISSS says DHS's SEVP "has recently issued updated guidance," that it "may impact CPT eligibility," and that "ISSS is pausing CPT applications." A student with an experience "required for their degree" should contact ISSS for instructions "if applicable." The pages say the content "may be outdated and will be updated soon," and were last updated September 3.
2. **The usual process**
	The CPT must be on the I-20 before work starts. A student first completes the ISSS CPT online workshop and then applies through MyISSS. ISSS cannot change, void, or restore CPT once authorized.
3. **Three categories on the special situations page**
	Required internship, co-op, and "Elective Internship/Employment." The last needs the instructor's authorization form. It is the category the August guidance removes. Extensions of up to 30 days need a letter, and longer ones need a new request.
4. **The CSE co-op route**
	The College of Science and Engineering co-op runs CPT through a co-op course, CSE 4896, which the student must be registered for before ISSS approves. The CSE page quotes about 10 business days at ISSS plus 2 to 5 at the co-op office, carries the same pause banner, and says to start the application only after accepting an offer.
## What Is Left For A CS Major
I understand my degree has no required internship, which I have not yet verified with the department. If that holds, no CPT is available to me today. It would return in three ways: a court order that restores the credit pathway, a UMN curriculum change that makes a practical-training course mandatory for everyone in a program, or DHS withdrawing the guidance. None is scheduled except the November 6 hearing ([[Junior Year Internship Decision]]).
## Failure Modes
- Starting work on an offer letter before the endorsed I-20 exists.
- Assuming an elective for-credit course still qualifies because it did last year.
- Treating the lawsuit as relief. A filing is not an order ([[Visa Litigation Tracker]]).
- Accumulating a year of full-time CPT without knowing it ends post-completion OPT eligibility.
