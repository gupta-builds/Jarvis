## Read This First
==The summer 2027 problem is that CPT, the usual internship permit, is paused at UMN, and the only other open route before graduation costs OPT months, STEM eligibility, and possibly a $70,000 school fee, so I need her to tell me what UMN will actually allow and what students are doing.==
1. **Goal:** finish spring 2028, start OPT right after, get picked in the March 2028 H-1B lottery, and keep work authorization unbroken through October 1, 2028 and beyond with the STEM extension.
2. **Now:** UMN's ISSS says it is "pausing CPT applications" and will consider only experiences "required for their degree." I believe CS has no required internship, which I have not confirmed.
3. **Why:** in August the federal program that oversees school officials told them to approve CPT only when training is required of every student. Four education groups sued on October 5, and the hearing is **November 6 at 3:00 PM**. UMN is a member of one of the plaintiffs, the AAU.
4. **Fee threat:** on October 8 ICE proposed charging schools $70,000 for a student's first OPT and $30,000 for each later one. Comments close **November 9**. Schools may pass the cost on.
5. **My constraint:** I do not want to use pre-completion OPT before I graduate, because it is deducted from my 12 months, using all of it forfeits STEM OPT, and the proposed fee prices it at $100,000 combined versus $70,000 for post-graduation OPT alone.
6. **My plan:** three lanes for summer 2027: an on-campus research or lab job, internship applications that I accept only once the authorization route exists in writing, and portfolio work. Details in [[Junior Year Internship Decision]].
## What I Want From The Meeting
- A straight answer on what UMN allows for summer 2027, and what other UMN international CS students are actually doing.
- A path to on-campus roles and to employers who work with OPT and H-1B.
- The name and channel of whoever at ISSS answers the immigration-specific questions in writing, since a career professional is not my immigration advisor.
> [!NOTE]
> Tell her the meeting is being transcribed and get her agreement at the start. Ask what she can answer officially and what she will refer to an ISSS advisor, and treat anything she says about rules as UMN's practice, not legal advice.
## Facts To Have In My Head
- *CPT:* messages BCM 2608-01 (Aug 12) and 2608-02 (Aug 24) say CPT is allowed "only if its absence would make the attainment of a degree an impossibility and if the work associated with CPT is required for all students." An optional elective fails. The regulation did not change. The lawsuit says the old rule was credit-bearing OR required ([[CPT Broadcast Messages]], [[CPT Lawsuit]]).
- *Court status:* no stay or order yet. The government's response is due October 28. A win would plausibly cover UMN, but ISSS would still need time to restart.
- *OPT clocks:* file 90 days before to 60 days after the program end date. UMN wants the recommendation request in MyISSS 100 days before completion. All initial OPT must fit within 14 months of completion. USCIS data cited in the complaint showed only 80 percent of F-1 OPT applications done within five months as of September 28. Premium processing guarantees action in 30 business days ([[EAD and OPT Processing]]).
- *Pre-completion OPT cost:* one year part-time costs 6 months of post-completion OPT, one year full-time costs all 12. A 12-week full-time summer is about 3 months (my arithmetic) ([[Pre-Completion OPT]]).
- *OPT fee:* charged to the school before the DSO can enter the recommendation. DHS says schools may pass it to students or employers and models a case where schools recommend only STEM students. It would apply 60 days after a final rule ([[OPT Fee Proposal]]).
- *Fixed-end-date rule:* blocked by a court since September 14, on expedited appeal. If it returns, the OPT filing windows shrink from 60 to 30 days and I would likely need an extension-of-stay application, because the no-extension cutoff is written as March 18, 2027 ([[Duration of Status Rule]]).
- *STEM:* Computer Science (CIP 11.0701) is on the DHS list. The employer must use E-Verify and sign a training plan ([[STEM OPT Extension]]).
- *H-1B:* employers register in March at a wage level, and level IV gets four lottery entries to level I's one. FY2027 registration ran March 4 to 19 at $215. The $100,000 payment does not apply to an in-country change of status that is granted, and courts have blocked it anyway ([[H-1B Lottery]], [[H-1B 100K Payment]]).
- *My dates:* petition filed from April 1, 2028, start October 1, 2028, cap-gap bridges the gap ([[2028 Graduation Timeline]]).
## What I Do Not Know Yet
- Whether any UMN CS program has a required practical training course, and whether one could be created.
- Whether ISSS will restart CPT quickly after a ruling.
- Whether UMN would pay, pass on, or decline the OPT fee, and whether it is commenting.
- Current ISSS turnaround on OPT recommendations, and real USCIS approval times.
- Whether remote work abroad, unpaid internships, or particular research jobs are allowed.
- Which employers hiring UMN CS students are E-Verify employers, register for H-1B, and at what wage level.
## Questions
Mark each with a check as it is answered. If time is short, ask only the ones marked ★ in order.
### Set 1: CPT And Summer 2027
1. ★ Is UMN authorizing any CPT right now, including through the CSE Co-op course CSE 4896, and is the pause on all CPT or only for-credit electives? *Why: tells me if any CPT door exists.*
2. ★ Has anyone at UMN or CSE considered making a practical training course required for every student in a program, since that is the one thing the federal guidance allows? Who would decide, and is there a timeline? *Why: the only structural way CPT returns for my major.*
3. ★ What are other international CS students at UMN doing for summer 2027 right now, especially those who already hold offers that assumed CPT? *Why: shows the real options, not the rule book.*
4. If the November 6 hearing produces a stay, how quickly would UMN restart CPT, and what would students need to do? *Why: sets how long to wait before choosing another lane.*
5. What happened to students whose CPT started before August? *Why: tells me how ISSS treats existing authorizations.*
### Set 2: Pre-Completion OPT And The Fee
6. ★ Is ISSS recommending pre-completion OPT this year, and what is the current turnaround from request to recommendation, and from filing to card? *Why: decides whether it is even feasible for a June start.*
7. ★ If the $70,000 OPT fee is finalized, has UMN said whether it would pay, pass it to students or employers, or limit OPT in some way? Who decides, and is UMN submitting a comment by November 9? *Why: the biggest threat to my 2028 OPT.*
8. Does ISSS advise students to avoid pre-completion OPT to preserve months and STEM eligibility, and is a short deduction of about three months ever worth it? *Why: tests my own decision rule.*
9. Would ISSS tell me in advance if it plans to stop recommending OPT for some students? *Why: early warning.*
### Set 3: Campus Work
10. ★ Which UMN summer roles count as on-campus employment for an international student: research assistants, lab jobs, department roles? Where are they posted, when do they hire, and can they be full-time in the summer? *Why: this is my only fully controllable lane.*
11. Does a project with an industry partner or an off-campus lab qualify, or only research tied to the curriculum or to contracted post-graduate research as the regulation says? *Why: avoids an unauthorized job by mistake.*
12. Can a faculty-supervised research project give credit and employment together without becoming CPT? *Why: tests an edge case.*
### Set 4: Employers And Sponsorship
13. ★ How are employers reacting to the CPT pause? Are companies still hiring UMN international students for summer 2027, deferring start dates, or offering other arrangements? *Why: decides how hard to chase internships.*
14. ★ How should I answer "Are you authorized to work in the U.S.?" and "Will you require sponsorship?" on applications when my route is CPT or OPT? *Why: a wrong answer can cost an offer or create a record problem.*
15. Which employers hiring UMN CS graduates are E-Verify employers who sign STEM training plans, register for H-1B, and at what wage level? Does Career Services track this? *Why: the STEM extension and the lottery depend on the employer.*
16. Can an offer be made contingent on work authorization, and how do employers handle a start date that might slip? *Why: protects me from accepting an offer I cannot start.*
17. What do UMN international CS alumni typically do after graduation, and how many got H-1B selection? Are cap-exempt employers like universities and research labs a common route? *Why: gives base rates.*
### Set 5: Graduation Process
18. ★ What is the program end date on my I-20, and what counts as the end date: last class, or degree conferral? When exactly can I request the OPT recommendation, and can I ask earlier than 100 days? *Why: every window counts from this date.*
19. What does the OPT workshop cover, and what turnaround are students seeing this year? *Why: planning.*
20. What start date do students usually request, right after graduation or later, given the 14-month limit? *Why: the 14-month rule makes delay costly.*
21. How does ISSS support students once they are selected for H-1B, and does it handle cap-gap I-20 updates? *Why: confirms who does what in 2028.*
### Set 6: Rule Changes And Communication
22. ★ How will ISSS communicate rule changes, through the Weekly Update, MyISSS, or sessions? Can I get alerts for the CPT ruling? *Why: stops me depending on blogs.*
23. If the fixed-end-date rule returns, what is ISSS's plan for students graduating in 2028, including the March 18, 2027 filing cutoff and travel advice? *Why: it would change my OPT paperwork.*
24. Should I avoid international travel in the coming year? *Why: travel can change admission terms if that rule returns.*
25. Is UMN a member of the Presidents' Alliance or NAFSA, and is the university supporting the CPT lawsuit? *Why: closes an open question on whether a ruling would cover UMN by two routes.*
### Set 7: If Time Allows
26. Is remote work done entirely outside the U.S. for a foreign or U.S. employer allowed, and what does it do to status and re-entry? *Why: no source I found answers it.*
27. Do unpaid or volunteer internships need authorization? *Why: ISSS lists benefits in kind as employment.*
28. Who is the best person at ISSS to answer questions 1, 6, 7, 18, 23 and 26 in writing, and what is the best way to reach them?
## If She Says... Then Ask
- *"CPT is paused, nothing we can do."* Then ask 2 (a required course) and 3 (what students do instead).
- *"Use pre-completion OPT."* Then ask 6 (turnaround), 7 (fee), and what it does to the 12 months and STEM.
- *"We don't know about the fee."* Then ask who would decide and when it will be announced.
- *"Talk to an advisor."* Then ask 28 and request a written reply date.
- *"Employers are still hiring."* Then ask which ones, and how they handle authorization timing.
