## What Is Established About CPT
==The regulation did not change; the guidance did; a court has not touched the guidance; and both sides leave schools one opening, a training that is truly required of everyone in a program.==
### Settled
1. **The regulation is unchanged.** 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i), July 1, 2026 version, still says "an integral part of an established curriculum" and lists "required internship or practicum" ([[CPT Program]]).
2. **The guidance is in force.** BCM 2608-01 (Aug 12) and BCM 2608-02 (Aug 24) tell DSOs to authorize CPT only when the training is required of all students in the degree, and an optional elective fails. Both say they are not rules ([[CPT Broadcast Messages]]).
3. **The school defines the requirement.** BCM 2608-01 says "the school, not the U.S. government, defines what is and is not required to complete a degree."
4. **The government's own brief leaves the opening.** Its October 28 filing says schools can keep authorizing CPT "provided the training is truly integral to their program of study" and sends other training to OPT ([[CPT Lawsuit]]).
5. **UMN has paused.** The ISSS CPT page and the CSE co-op international page both still say so on November 5.
### Not Settled
1. When the judge rules, since the hearing is over and no date was announced, and how wide any relief reaches. UMN belongs to the AAU, one of four plaintiffs, but the relief asked for covers members and their students, and the judge decides the scope.
2. Whether and how fast ISSS restarts after a ruling. The advisor said on October 23 she had "genuinely no idea."
3. Whether any UMN program has, or could create, a training required of every student.
4. How ISSS treats CPT already on an I-20 from before August.
### The One Structural Path
BCM 2608-02 says all students, foreign and American, must do the training if the curriculum makes it integral, and that the employer needs a cooperative agreement with the school. A CS elective, and the optional CSE co-op, fail that test as written. A required practicum or co-op for every student in a program would pass it. That is a curriculum decision by the department and college, so ISSS cannot make it alone, and I need to find out who can.
### What This Means For Summer 2027
I plan as if CPT does not return before the March 2027 decision point and treat a ruling as upside ([[Junior Year Internship Decision]]). A stay would also not make UMN restart on its own, as [[SEVP and DSOs]] explains.

## Questions
Mark each with a check as it is answered. If time is short, ask only the ones marked ★ in order.
### Set 1: CPT
1. ★ On October 23 the pause was described as covering "elective" CPT, but the ISSS page says all applications are paused, with an exception for experiences "required for their degree." Which is the policy, and will a written version be posted? *Why: the two descriptions give different answers for an elective course.*
2. ★ Does any UMN program, including Computer Science or the CSE co-op through CSE 4896, require a practical training experience of every student? If not, has anyone proposed it, who would decide, and is there a timeline? *Why: the only structural way CPT returns for my major.*
3. ★ If the court grants a stay or injunction, does ISSS restart immediately, wait for UMN counsel or SEVP, or wait to see whether the order reaches UMN? How will students be told? *Why: sets how long I wait before choosing another lane.*
4. What happens to CPT already on an I-20 from before August? *Why: shows how ISSS reads the guidance on past approvals.*
### Set 2: Pre-Completion OPT
5. ★ ISSS said it would announce its pre-completion request process in Fall 2026. When, where, and what turnaround should I plan for at ISSS and at USCIS? *Why: a June start needs the request about March 8.*
6. ★ Does ISSS recommend a short full-time summer stretch only, and keeping at least 9 months for after graduation? *Why: tests my own rule in [[Pre-Completion OPT]].*
7. Will ISSS tell students early if it plans to limit OPT recommendations? *Why: the fee could change what ISSS can do.*
### Set 3: The OPT Fee
8. ★ Is UMN, or the AAU or NAFSA on its behalf, filing a comment by Monday November 9, and can students see it? *Why: the comment record is what DHS must answer.*
9. ★ If the fee is finalized, has UMN said whether it would pay, pass it on, or limit OPT? Who decides? *Why: the biggest threat to my 2028 OPT ([[OPT Fee Proposal]]).*
10. Is a student comment useful, and is there guidance on what to include? Comments are public. *Why: decides whether I file one.*

13. Is a remote role for a U.S. employer, done while I am physically abroad, treated the same way? *Why: no source I read answers it.*
14. Can ISSS give me an example of safe resume wording for projects and research? *Why: the advisor's strongest warning ([[OPT Playbook]]).*
### Set 5: Rule Watch And Records
15. ★ How will ISSS communicate the CPT ruling, and can I get the notice directly? *Why: stops me depending on blogs.*
16. If the fixed-end-date rule returns, what is the plan for 2028 graduates, and should I avoid travel this year? *Why: it would change my OPT paperwork ([[Duration of Status Rule]]).*
