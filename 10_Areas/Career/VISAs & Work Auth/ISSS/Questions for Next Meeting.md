# Questions for Next Meeting
This is the question sheet for my last ISSS meeting this semester about the duration of status rule. It exists to find out what UMN will do about two court rulings and what it is waiting for, and to get every answer settled and written down. Read "Train Of Thought" and "Questions" the night before, and keep the ★ questions open during the call.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This meeting's job is to learn UMN's position and its triggers, not to ask for action.** ISSS's email already settled whether and when CPT restarts, so this meeting asks what UMN does if the orders are reversed, how the CPT restart fits my summer 2027 plan, and what UMN does and says about the duration of status rule while the government decides whether to appeal. The full CPT procedure still waits for the spring meeting.

> [!SUCCESS]
> **ISSS's email arrived (received by Nov 19) and answered two of my biggest questions.** The duration of status rule "continues to not be in effect," and ISSS will keep monitoring for an appeal and post updates on its hub. Below, each question is marked answered, partly answered, or still open.
## What The Meeting Must Leave Me With
1. **UMN's written position** on the duration of status ruling and on the CPT order.

3. **What I do and do not do** between now and spring: travel, filings, and any internship offer.
## Train Of Thought
The order of the questions follows one chain of reasoning, so I can improvise when an answer changes the path.
1. **The judges have spoken, and both orders are in force.** Judge Saylor vacated the duration of status rule on November 16, and Judge Kobick stayed the August CPT messages on November 17. Neither is permanent, because the government has until about January 15 and January 16, 2027 to appeal ([[Duration of Status Rule]], [[CPT Lawsuit]]).
2. **So the next decisions are not the judges'.** They belong to UMN and to the government. A school acts on what its own officials, its lawyers, and its SEVP representative tell it. The Reddit DSO I read shows how uneven that is: his SEVP representative told him to cancel existing CPT, while other schools said they never rescinded approved CPT. UMN paused CPT but did not revoke approvals, which is a third posture.
3. **UMN did not wait.** ISSS resumes CPT on November 23 even though the appeal window is open, so its trigger was the court order, not the end of the appeal period. That moves the useful question to what UMN does if an appeal reverses the order, because the Reddit DSO's fear was having to make students quit mid-semester.
4. **For me the duration of status risk is narrow.** The rule only matters to my 2028 graduation if the government wins an appeal or writes a new rule. So I need two things: how UMN will tell students if that starts to happen, and whether to avoid anything this winter that a revived rule would punish, mainly travel.
5. **What is left unresolved is institutional,** so the people who can answer are ISSS and, through ISSS, UMN's lawyers. I should not ask ISSS for legal predictions. I should ask what UMN has decided, what it is monitoring, and when it will tell students.

## Questions
Mark each with a check as it is answered. If time is short, ask only the ★ ones, in order.
### Set 1: The Duration Of Status Ruling
- [ ] **Q1 ★** How does ISSS read the November 16 ruling for UMN students today? Is duration of status fully back, with the 60-day grace period and the usual I-20 and I-94 process, and can I have that in writing? *Why: ISSS's pages still say "paused," so I need the plain current answer. Partly answered: the email says the rule "continues to not be in effect." Still ask for the 60-day grace period and the I-94 in writing.*
- [ ] **Q2 ★** What is UMN waiting for before it treats the ruling as settled: the appeal deadline in mid-January, a notice of appeal, or a message from SEVP? When does ISSS expect to know? *Why: this names the trigger and the date. Partly answered: the email says ISSS is monitoring for an appeal and will post updates on the hub, but it names no date or trigger.*
- [ ] **Q3 ★** If the government appeals and asks for a stay, how fast will ISSS tell students, through which channel, and will it tell us before a stay is granted or only after? *Why: it sets how much warning I would get. Partly answered: updates go on the hub and in emails, with no timing given.*
- [ ] **Q4 ★** Should I travel over winter break? If I leave and return, do I come back on "D/S," and what does ISSS advise if a stay revived the rule while I was abroad? *Why: a revived rule would give a returning student a fixed admission, which is the one trap in the rule.*
- [ ] **Q5** Will ISSS update the duration of status hub and the OPT pages, and when? Which pages should I treat as current until then? *Why: the pages say they are valid only until the rule takes effect. The hub still showed its Sept 15 text on Nov 19, while the CPT page was updated Nov 18.*
- [ ] **Q6** Has SEVP, or UMN's SEVP representative, said anything to UMN about the ruling? *Why: SEVP representatives told some schools different things about CPT, so I want to know whether UMN received any instruction on duration of status.*
- [ ] **Q7** What did UMN prepare for the fixed-period rule, such as extension-of-stay support or I-20 changes, and does it keep that work in case the rule returns? *Why: it shows how ready UMN is for a revival.*
