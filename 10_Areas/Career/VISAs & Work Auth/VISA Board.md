# VISA Board
## Purpose
This folder holds everything I need to go from F-1 student at UMN to a paid U.S. job without a gap in work authorization, in a year when most of the rules are being rewritten in court. Read this board to find the right note, and read [[VISA Plan]] to know what to do next.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **One rule for the whole folder:** [[VISA Plan]] is the only note that tells me what to do and when. Every other note either explains a rule, tracks a court case, or records a meeting, and it feeds the plan. If a note and the plan disagree, the plan is wrong until I check the source and fix it.
## Map
### How To Read The Folder
Start with [[VISA Plan]], which is the dated route from now to the end of my first OPT year, with the procedures, costs, and decision rules for each step. Everything else is a reason behind one of its rows. When a row says "see" a note, that note holds the proof, the source links, and the detail the plan leaves out. If a word is new, [[US Immigration Rules Explained]] defines it from zero, and it is the right first read for anyone who has never seen an I-20.
The notes sit in four layers. Read down the layers when you need more depth, and read up when something goes stale.
| Layer | What it holds | Notes | How often it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan** | What I do, date by date | [[VISA Plan]], [[2028 Graduation Timeline]], [[Junior Year Internship Decision]] | Every week or two |
| **Watch** | Court cases, dates, and primary sources | [[Visa Litigation Tracker]], [[Current News]] | Every time a ruling lands |
| **Research** | One government action explained in depth | [[Duration of Status Rule]], [[CPT Lawsuit]], [[CPT Broadcast Messages]], [[OPT Fee Proposal]], [[H-1B 100K Payment]], [[H-1B Rule Proposals]] | When that action moves |
| **Reference** | Rules that rarely change | [[F-1 Visa]], [[OPT Program]], [[CPT Program]], [[H1B Visa]], [[SEVP and DSOs]], [[F-1 Employment Options]], [[US Immigration Rules Explained]] | Rarely |
### The Plan And What Feeds It
The plan's OPT track is the most detailed part, and it rests on three notes. [[OPT Playbook]] holds the three kinds of OPT side by side, the costs, and the mistakes that cost students their eligibility. [[EAD and OPT Processing]] explains the work permit card, the five-month wait, premium processing, and the 14-month rule that turns delay into lost work months. [[STEM OPT Extension]] and [[Cap-Gap Extension]] cover what comes after the first twelve months. [[Pre-Completion OPT]] covers the summer 2027 option, and [[Junior Year Internship Decision]] explains how I choose between it, CPT, on-campus research, and an internship abroad.
The plan's CPT track rests on [[CPT Program]], which explains how CPT works and how UMN ran it before August, and on [[CPT Broadcast Messages]], which records the two emails that stopped it. [[CPT Lawsuit]] records what the judge ordered on November 17 and why.
The H-1B track starts in March 2028, before I graduate. [[H-1B Odds Plan]] turns the lottery into a list of choices I control, [[H-1B Lottery]] holds the mechanics, and [[H1B Visa]] holds the basic rules.
### The Watch Layer
Four things could change the plan, and each has a home. A court cancelled the duration of status rule on November 16, and [[Duration of Status Rule]] records what it said, what happens if the government appeals, and what I would do if it returned. The proposed $70,000 school fee is in [[OPT Fee Proposal]], and [[H-1B 100K Payment]] holds the proclamation that affects the H-1B step. Every case and its next date sit in [[Visa Litigation Tracker]], and the primary sources behind them sit in [[Current News]]. Both go stale first, so check them before trusting a date elsewhere.
### The ISSS Meetings
ISSS is the UMN office that signs my paperwork, so its answers are the only ones that bind my school. [[ISSS Meeting]] holds the research behind my first meeting on October 23, [[October Meeting Transcript]] holds what the advisor said word for word, and [[ISSS October Meeting Brief]] summarizes it. [[ISSS November Meeting Prep]] holds the CPT and pre-completion questions asked before the rulings, and [[Questions for Next Meeting]] plans the last meeting this semester, which is about what UMN will do and what it is waiting for.
