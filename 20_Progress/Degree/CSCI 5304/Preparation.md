# CSCI 5304 — Preparation
==Quizzes are 30% of the grade and happen every other Thursday testing the prior two weeks - the single highest-leverage habit here is a two-week study cycle that ends the Wednesday before each quiz, not a scramble the morning of.==
## Where the Points Actually Are
Exams (40%) and Quizzes (30%) together are 70% of the grade - Homework (20%) and Attendance & Participation (10%) are real but secondary. Quizzes are the more frequent, lower-stakes-each-time lever: six quizzes at roughly 5% each versus two exams at 20% each. Missing one quiz is recoverable; missing exam prep is not - treat exam study as a compounding review of every prior quiz cycle, not a fresh two-week cram.
## Cheapest Points
Attendance & Participation (10%) has no separate attendance sheet - it's judged through in-class discussion, assignments, and the quizzes themselves. Showing up and engaging on Tuesday/Thursday earns this automatically alongside the harder work; there's no separate participation assignment to remember.
## The Real Traps
- **The `environment.yml` scikit-learn gap** - the README's own verify command imports `sklearn`, but `scikit-learn` isn't in the environment file. Fix this before the first coding homework, not after the import fails mid-assignment.
- **Zero-AI-tools policy, stated twice, with real teeth** - a 0 on the assignment for a first offense, a university report for repeats or anything egregious. This is understanding-only territory for AI help, not homework-code or quiz-prep-answer territory - see [[CSCI 5304 Board]]'s Academic Integrity section.
- **Late work gets a zero, full stop**, unless the instructor is contacted *before* the deadline for a valid reason - no after-the-fact grace built into this policy, unlike [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 4511W/CSCI 4511W Board|CSCI 4511W]]'s automatic one-day grace window or the now-archived [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 3081W/CSCI 3081W Board|CSCI 3081W]]'s automatic-drop cushion.
- **The printed schedule has three real date anomalies** (documented in the Board note) - don't trust the Week 6+ dates, the midterm date, or the "Holiday Break" placement without a Canvas check first.
## Study Cadence
Two-week block ending the Wednesday before each Thursday quiz: read the textbook Lectures covered in that block (Trefethen & Bau numbering matches the course's own lecture numbers - see [[CSCI 5304 Board]]'s Course Content section), redo the homework problems from that block instead of just confirming they were submitted, and use office hours (Tue/Thu 9:30-11:00am - same days as class) the week of the quiz for anything that didn't fully land in lecture.
