# ENGL 1004 (003) — Preparation
==Journal Entries (30%) and Class Participation (20%) together are half the grade, and both are graded on engagement quality, not correctness - this is the opposite of a course where showing up quietly is enough.==
## Where the Points Actually Are
Journal Entries (30%), Simulation (20%), and Participation (20%) together are 70% of the grade - Role Creation (10%) and the Final Exam (20%) are the remaining 30%. Unlike a typical lit course, there's no single high-stakes paper - the grade is built almost entirely from sustained, real engagement across the semester rather than a few big deliverables.
## Cheapest Points
There isn't really a free-points category here - even attendance-adjacent credit (Class Participation) explicitly requires visible engagement, not just presence, per [[ENGL 1004 Board]]. The closest thing to a forgiving margin: Journal Entries score a 1 (not a 0) for being late up to a week, so a missed entry isn't catastrophic if made up promptly.
## The Real Traps
- **Attendance has a hard failure threshold, not just a grade penalty**: one cumulative unexcused week lowers the grade, three cumulative weeks fails the course outright.
- **Electronic device policy has real teeth**: first phone-out gets a warning, second gets an automatic 0 for that day's participation - stated as deliberate, not an idle threat.
- **Journal entries must be handwritten and submitted as a photo/scan**, due at least 1 hour before class - a typed entry or a late-night submission the morning of class both fail the format/timing requirement even if the content is strong.
- **Content warning is real, not procedural** - the reading list includes *The Handmaid's Tale*, *The Bluest Eye*, and *Maus*, texts genuinely challenged/banned in real districts, and the syllabus states outright that students who'd be unable to participate around difficult material shouldn't be in the course.
## Study Cadence
Buy all 8 required texts immediately - the first reading (*Huckleberry Finn* ch. 1-6) is due 9/15, only a week into the semester, and the pace doesn't let up. The simulation block (10/8, 10/13, 10/20) is the structural center of the course - role creation work done well before 10/1 (when the revised role sketch is due) pays off across three full class sessions plus the debrief, not just one assignment.
## Weekly Study Plan
> [!WARNING] No workload statement exists in this syllabus - the figure below is an estimate, not a stated fact
> Unlike [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 4061/CSCI 4061 Board|CSCI 4061]] or [[20_Progress/Degree/MGMT 3015/MGMT 3015 Board|MGMT 3015]], [[ENGL 1004 Board]] never states an expected hours/week figure. Estimated via the standard UMN 3 hrs/credit/week convention (3 credits x 3 = **~9 hours/week total**, of which ~2.5 hrs is the two class sessions, leaving ~6.5 hrs for reading, journaling, and simulation prep outside class). Given 8 full books and 12 handwritten journal entries across the semester, real weeks - especially the three-session simulation block and the *Handmaid's Tale*/*Huckleberry Finn* stretches - may run heavier than this generic estimate. Don't treat 9 hours as a ceiling.
