## Grading Criteria
1. **CSCI 4061** (full syllabus and schedule: [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 4061/CSCI 4061 Board|CSCI 4061 Board]]):
	- Weekly Canvas Quizzes (lowest 2 dropped): 10%
	- Laboratory Assignments (lowest 2 dropped): 10%
	- Course Projects (4 total, each with an individual oral exam): 25%
	- Midterm Exam 1 (Thu 10/8, in class): 15%
	- Midterm Exam 2 (Thu 11/12, in class): 15%
	- Final Exam, cumulative (Section 001: Mon 12/21; Section 010: Thu 12/17): 20%
	- Surveys: 5%
	- 93%+ for an A, no rounding. ~12 hrs/week workload - the heaviest of the five active Fall'26 classes. AI tools are banned from producing any submitted lab/project code - allowed only for learning concepts, debugging investigation, or unrelated examples.
2. **CSCI 4511W - Introduction to Artificial Intelligence** (full syllabus: [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 4511W/CSCI 4511W Board|CSCI 4511W Board]]) - replaced CSCI 3081W 2026-09-09:
	- Problem Sets (4): 36%
	- Long Quizzes (3): 24%
	- Short Quizzes (12-14): 12%
	- Writing Assignments (4): 12%
	- Final Project: 10%
	- Discussion Attendance & Participation: 6%
	- 90%+ for some level of A, 80%+ B, 70%+ C, 60%+ D - no finer +/- breakdown given. Real week-by-week schedule and full late-work policy not yet captured from Canvas.
3. **MGMT 3015 (001) - Introduction to Entrepreneurship** (full syllabus: [[20_Progress/Degree/MGMT 3015/MGMT 3015 Board|MGMT 3015 Board]]):
	- Profile of a Successful Entrepreneur (individual): 5%
	- New Business Idea (individual): 5%
	- Quiz 1 (individual): 10%
	- Business Idea Pitches (group): 10%
	- Reflection on Idea Pitches (individual): 5%
	- Quiz 2 (individual): 10%
	- Final Presentation (group): 10%
	- Written Business Plan (group): 25%
	- Class Participation (individual): 20%
	- 6-8 hrs/week workload. AI (ChatGPT etc.) explicitly encouraged with credit/accuracy caveats - the only Fall'26 course that permissive, but reports still can't be AI-written.
4. **CSCI 4521 - Applied Machine Learning** (full syllabus: [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 4521/CSCI 4521 Board|CSCI 4521 Board]]) - source syllabus is dated Fall 2025, unconfirmed current for this semester, see the Board note's warning:
	- Homework 1-4: 40%
	- Quizzes 1-6: 30%
	- Final Project: 20%
	- Participation Activities: 10%
	- Called "approximate" by the syllabus itself, unlike any other Fall'26 course's grading table. No date-anchored schedule exists yet - only 5 topic units with no calendar. Per-assignment color-coded AI policy (green/yellow/red) rather than one blanket rule.
5. **CSCI 5304**: real syllabus and schedule already captured in full (built by a separate pass this session, not yet cross-linked here): [[20_Progress/Degree/CSCI 5304/CSCI 5304 Board|CSCI 5304 Board]] - Attendance & Participation 10%, Homework 20%, Quizzes 30%, Exams (midterm + final) 40%. Zero-AI-tools policy, stated twice, with real teeth (0 on the assignment, university report for repeats).
