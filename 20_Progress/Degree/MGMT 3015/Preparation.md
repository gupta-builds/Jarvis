# MGMT 3015 (001) — Preparation
==Group deliverables (Business Idea Pitches, Final Presentation, Written Business Plan) are 45% of the grade combined, and Class Participation alone is 20% - together that's 65% riding on team quality and consistent live engagement, not on any single written assignment.==
## Where the Points Actually Are
Written Business Plan (25%) is the single largest component, followed by Class Participation (20%) - together 45% from just two lines. Add the two group presentations (Business Idea Pitches 10%, Final Presentation 10%) and group/team-dependent work is **45% of the total grade**, with individual writing (Profile 5%, New Business Idea 5%, Reflection 5%) and the two Quizzes (10% each = 20%) making up the rest. Picking a functional team early matters more here than in most Fall'26 courses - the Written Business Plan alone is worth as much as both quizzes combined.
## Cheapest Points
Class Participation (20%) has no separate exam or rubric-graded deliverable behind it - it's earned through the behaviors [[MGMT 3015 Board]] states directly (on time, full session, comments tied to real concepts). This is real, sustained effort but not a skill-execution risk the way a written plan or a graded quiz is - showing up prepared every session captures a fifth of the grade with no single point of failure.
## The Real Traps
- **"Course assignments are not weighted" on the syllabus page is a Canvas artifact, not real policy** - the actual grading table (in the separate Course Overview page) sums cleanly to 100% and is the one to plan against. See [[MGMT 3015 Board]]'s Grading warning.
- **Quiz make-ups need two weeks' notice, not a same-week email** - this is stricter than it sounds; a documented reason discovered the week of the quiz is already too late to guarantee a make-up slot.
- **Required email subject tag `[MGMT 3015: 3]`** - easy to forget and functionally invisible until a real email needs a fast reply and gets buried or delayed for missing it.
- **The Written Business Plan and Final Presentation share the same 12/16 deadline** regardless of which day your group actually presents (Group A or Group B) - per [[MGMT 3015 Board]]'s Schedule fragment, presenting earlier (12/14) doesn't buy any extra time on the plan itself.
- **The specific textbook edition matters** - "5th ed." is stated explicitly; multiple editions exist and the syllabus puts the burden on the student to master the bookstore's specific edition, not whichever is cheapest online.
## Study Cadence
6-8 hours/week outside class, unevenly distributed per [[MGMT 3015 Board]]'s Time Commitment section - lighter weeks (reading, news-scanning, networking, idea-journal entries) alternate with heavier weeks (case/quiz prep, venture planning, presentation/plan work). The two Quizzes (10/7 and 12/2) cover readings, cases, slides, *and* class discussion - meaning quiz prep can't be done from the textbook alone, live class attendance is load-bearing for exam content in a way it isn't in a pure-lecture course.
## Weekly Study Plan
**6-8 hours/week outside class**, per [[MGMT 3015 Board]]'s own Time Commitment section - stated as its own figure, not a 3hrs/credit estimate. Add the ~3.3 hrs/week actually in the two class sessions (2:30-4:10pm, Mon/Wed) for a realistic total of **~9-11 hours/week**. Lighter weeks: reading, news-scanning, networking, idea-journal entries. Heavier weeks: case/quiz prep, venture planning, presentation and business-plan work - concentrate extra hours around the two Quiz weeks and the group-deliverable weeks (pitches, final presentation, written plan) below.
