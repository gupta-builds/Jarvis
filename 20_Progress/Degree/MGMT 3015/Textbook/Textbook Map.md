## Why the Out-of-Order Sequence Is Worth Noting
The Business Plan (Ch 8) is taught in Module 4, *before* Industry Analysis, Strategy, and Business Models (Ch 6, 6, 5 in Module 5) - meaning students draft plan mechanics before covering some of the analytical content that would normally inform a plan's content. This isn't a data error; it's confirmed by two independent module titles ("Module 4: The Business Plan & Quiz 1" then "Module 5: Industry Analysis, Strategy & Business Models") and matches the course's own stated hands-on philosophy in [[MGMT 3015 Board]] - start building the plan early, layer in the analytical tools as the semester continues, rather than front-loading all theory before any real drafting starts.
