# System Design
==No start date. This track waits on purpose - see Why This One before treating that as a gap.==

## What This Is
[donnemartin/system-design-primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) - a curated open-source study guide, not a structured course. No enrollment, no certificate, no exam. It's a GitHub repo people read through and practice against: a topic index (performance vs. scalability, latency vs. throughput, availability vs. consistency including CAP theorem, consistency patterns, availability patterns, DNS, CDN, load balancers, reverse proxies, the application layer, databases, caching, asynchronism, communication, security), a four-step interview methodology, 8 worked system-design interview problems with solutions (Pastebin/Bit.ly, Twitter timeline/search, a web crawler, Mint.com, social network data structures, a key-value store, Amazon sales ranking, scaling to millions on AWS), 7 object-oriented design problems (hash map, LRU cache, call center, deck of cards, parking lot, chat server, circular array), and three Anki flashcard decks (system design, system design exercises, OO design exercises). The normal way through it: read the topic index once for the concepts, then work the interview questions as if they were LeetCode problems - write an approach, check it against the given solution, note what you missed.

## Why This One
System design rounds are a later-stage interview gate, not a screen. They show up in onsite loops and final rounds at the companies [[10_Areas/Life/Plans/Fall 2026/Fall 2026 - The One Thing|Fall 2026 - The One Thing]] is actually targeting, after a resume passes and after a coding screen clears. Starting this before GitHub Foundations, AI Associate Engineer, and the LeetCode/CodePath floor are stable would spread the same weekly hours across four unstabilized habits instead of three - the classic failure mode already documented in this vault: [[10_Areas/Life/Plans/Summer 2026/LeetCode & CSCI 4041|LeetCode & CSCI 4041]] was fully designed all summer and logged zero rows, because too much got started at once with no single thing actually running daily. Waiting here is the corrective, not neglect. The real risk isn't starting late - the September/October applications this plan is racing to send won't reach onsite rounds until October/November at the earliest, so there's real runway.

- **Study Guide** - the repo's own recommended prep path by timeline (short/medium/long), worth reading first to pick a lane instead of reading linearly.
- Also present: Anki decks (3), multiple language translations, a contributing guide, real-world architecture references, and a company engineering blogs section - lower priority than the topic index and problem sets.
