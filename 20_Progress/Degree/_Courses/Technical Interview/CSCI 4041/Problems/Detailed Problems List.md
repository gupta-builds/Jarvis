A blank Easy or Medium cell means the Pareto set didn't cover that day — pull from **Grind75** (techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75), filtered to the week's topic, instead.
- **Hard (3)** and **Interview-Based (2)** are blank everywhere below — pull Hard from **liquidslr/leetcode-company-wise-problems**, filtered to hard and to whatever company is actually being targeted that week; pull Interview-Based from **coding-interview-university**'s matching topic section plus Grind75's own cheatsheet/behavioral material. - "Resource pool" per week names the specific filter to apply, not a new source.
- Day 1–7 slots take one problem link + name each once solved; the `DSA Common To Share All` folder is fully dropped from this build — none of its files are used.
