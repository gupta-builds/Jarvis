This is the thread to mine for questions. Jesse interned at **Seagate** in 2007 and graduated from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities in 2008 in Computer Engineering. His own account of the niche: people who are technically strong and can turn a client's problem into requirements, keeping it high level with executives. His team is about 100 people in a company of about 3,000, and clients include 3M, Cargill, Seagate and General Mills. Opinions: AI in plants mostly serves plant managers and above, now reaching operators; vision AI handles safety checks; and he prefers finding and repeating the best shifts ("golden batches") to hunting for faults. He mentors because he enjoys it and was an admissions ambassador himself ([[Mentor Details'26]], [[Initial Outreach]]).
