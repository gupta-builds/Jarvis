A polished pitch mattered as much as the build itself. That lesson carries forward directly into Goal 3 below.
### The 80, Three Committed Goals
These are the goals that must be true by the end of this program year, each with its own executional steps, not just an intent:
1. **Internships.** Land a 2027-cycle internship at a large AI-forward or FAANG-tier company, backed by technical interview readiness, an active LinkedIn presence, conference attendance, a stronger reference network, real application volume, and a resume and portfolio that have been reviewed and changed, not just discussed. This directly answers the CPT/OPT bind left unresolved by the Ahnaf relationship, see [[Plan - Ahnaf#Current State|Plan]].
2. **Project mentorship for startups and project management.** Get third-person, outside feedback on AI systems and project work (Jarvis, CausalOps, TradingView), VC-level startup advice, startup idea generation, project reviews, and communication and management judgment, the same full-lifecycle ownership lens Jesse brings from SymphonyAI.
3. **Marketing.** Build real communication, selling, and idea-generation skill, plus the practical shortcuts experienced operators actually use to run productive meetings and pitch clearly. This is the skill the hackathon retrospective flagged as equally important to the build itself.
### The 20, Stretch Goals
Capped at five on purpose, so stretch work never grows into a second set of main goals. Full detail in [[Smart Goals]]; compressed here:
1. **LinkedIn, network, and content maxing** (Goal 1 and the content half of Goal 3): 500+ connections, 1,000+ followers, regular posts, with portfolio blog posts folded into this same push rather than tracked separately.
2. **Mock interview and technical-prep cadence** (Goal 1): a recurring mock-interview rhythm, coding and system design, running through 2027, with regular LeetCode practice folded in rather than tracked separately.
3. **Startup idea validation pass** (Goal 2): one real problem-thesis and validation exercise with Jesse's input, mirroring the Winter "learn the playbook, no launch" approach from [[Plan - Ahnaf#Winter|Ahnaf's plan]], carried to an actual decision this time.
4. **In-person networking and event comfort** (Goal 1 and Goal 3 jointly): a concrete number of conferences or meetups attended per quarter, plus real tips from Jesse on working a room, not "network more" left vague.
5. **Open, reserved slot.** Not invented in advance, filled only once a real conversation with Jesse surfaces something worth tracking on its own.
