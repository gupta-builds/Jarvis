# Preparation Workflow
Turn the course's real grading structure into habits with triggers and done conditions.
## Use when
At course setup, after the first graded artifact, before each major assessment, and whenever the rules or schedule change.
## Steps
1. Read [[Preparation Standard]], the course Board, the schedule, and the assessment notes.
2. In Where the Points Actually Are, rank assessments by verified weight, frequency, difficulty, and secondary deadlines, with the arithmetic.
3. List Cheapest Points: the credit that costs the least effort per point.
4. Build Study Cadence from the real meeting and due-date schedule: pre-reading, textbook and concept notes, lecture capture, post-lecture reconciliation, assignments, discussion, review. Label estimates, and give each habit a trigger and a done condition.
5. Record The Real Traps with source and date, and label inference. Add Professor-Specific Notes only when stated, with `Verified`, `Observed repeatedly`, `Inferred`, or `Ask`.
6. Write the Weekly Study Plan against dates, with the preparation mode each assessment rule requires.
7. Revisit after feedback or a repeated failure, and change the habit that would have prevented it.
8. Run [[Vault Lint]] on the path.
## Done when
- Priorities follow verified course rules, not generic study advice.
- Habits produce observable notes, practice, or submissions.
- Estimates and uncertainty are labeled.
