# Career Fair Intake
Turn a recruiting event into captured sources, dossiers for real postings, and notes for the ones worth pursuing. Handshake and LinkedIn stay a manual lane through this Workflow; nothing automates a login.
## Use when
After a career fair, or when a batch of postings arrives from one event instead of the research loop. Everything here is by hand.
## Steps
1. Before the fair, list the companies in the fair note, one line each with the posting status and a question to bring.
2. During and after, paste raw conversations and pages as separate capture notes under `Career Fair/`, untouched. Captures are private.
3. For each posting you heard about, write one dossier in `List/Dossiers/_Career Fair/` with `source: "CareerFair-Manual"`, the `career-fair` tag, a specific `matched_reason`, and a `tier` (anything from this lane is at least tier 2).
4. Screen each dossier by reading the posting, and record `screened_decision` and `screened_reason`.
5. For each pass, follow [[Internship Pipeline]] from step 4: a Program in `Programs/Serious/Career Fair/` and a Contact in `Contacts/Each One/Ongoing/`, sharing one file name. No Tracker exists until the application is submitted.
6. Record the event and its counts in the fair note.
## Do not
Do not create the notes from a blank template without the posting's facts. Do not promote a posting that never went through Screen. Do not rewrite a capture after it is saved.
## Done when
- Every posting heard about has a dossier, or a line in the fair note saying why not.
- Every pass has its Program and Contact, cross-linked and named alike.
