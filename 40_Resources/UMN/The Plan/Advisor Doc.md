## 6. What A Junior Should Know About Graduating
*For me, and shareable.* ==The audit decides who graduates, so the APAS gets checked every term and the four-year plan is only a guide.== The plan PDF says so itself: it is not a contract.
- *Applying to graduate:* the link sits in MyU under Academics, Degree Progress, and appears once 90 degree credits are complete. I should be at 94 after Fall 2026 posts if all 18 pass and apply. Without applying, UMN schedules graduation automatically at 75% of degree-applicable credits. A wrong term is fixed with the Request to Change Expected Graduation Term form. Each term has its own deadline on the academic calendar.
- *Before applying:* check for holds, set the diploma name and address, and run the APAS.
- *Grade rules:* major courses must be A-F with C- or better, and a course counts for one major requirement only. S/N counts only toward general electives, and 3 of the 20 allowed S/N credits are used.
- *Residency:* the audit checks 30 credits and 15 of the last 30 through UMN. Fall 2026 satisfies both.
- *The last term has no recovery slot:* CSCI 3081W and Literature are the two requirements that cannot slip. A drop or a failed grade in either leaves no later regular term. This is my inference, so the advisor question in section 3 matters.
- *Waitlists:* they auto-register only when no time conflict exists, close the night before the term, and cannot be joined after the first day.
- *Registration priority:* students with 90 or more cumulative credits (completed plus in progress) register in the first stage. Spring 2027 Stage 1 ran November 10 to 17.
- *Withdrawals:* a W appears on the transcript from the second drop window onward. After the college-approval date, a drop needs the college.
- *Minor:* the 2026-11-18 audit has no minor section because the minor is undeclared. Check that it appears after admission.
- *Ceremony:* CSE runs its own undergraduate commencement and a graduation fair in spring. The 2027 ceremony is Thursday, May 13, 2027 at 3M Arena. The 2028 date is not published.
