## What Graduation Looks Like
==Only three degree requirements are deliberately postponed: **CSCI 3081W** (Fall'27), the **Literature** course (Spring'28), and the minor's last credits.==
- **After Fall'26:** 94 credits, 26 to go. In progress: CSCI 4061, 4511W, 4521, 5304 and MGMT 3015.
- **After Spring'27:** the technical-elective requirement is **one credit short** (22 of 23) and any Fall'27 list course closes it. Elective credits are complete. Still open: CSCI 3081W, Literature, one writing-intensive course, the minor.
- **After Fall'27:** **CSCI 3081W** closes the CS core and writing intensive. Only Literature and 1 minor credit are left.
- **After Spring'28:** Literature and the minor close. The audit should show a complete degree and a complete minor.
- **Postponing Literature and 3081W on purpose:** the technical requirement is full by Spring'27, so every later technical course counts toward 120 but not toward the technical requirement. Delaying the two required courses buys nothing except taking wanted courses earlier, and it moves risk into the last two terms. 3081W has no substitute except MADR 3081W, and Literature has many sections.
- **Extra credits:** about 32 beyond 120. They do not shorten the degree or convert into anything at UMN. They add courses, research credits and faculty contacts for grad school elsewhere (my reasoning, not a UMN rule).

## Courses Said NO
==The crucial CSCI courses below were dropped on judgment, not on an advisor's word, so each needs a second opinion before it is closed for good.==
- **CSCI 5105 · Introduction to Distributed Systems** (3, Spring only, needs CSCI 4061). Communication and RPC, clocks, mutual exclusion, replication, consensus, consistency and CAP, distributed file systems. Three team programming projects. *Why NO:* not interesting, and Cloud (5144) carries the applied side. *Counterpoint:* it is the one course that teaches consensus and replication directly. *Ask:* is 5144 enough?
- **CSCI 4211 Computer Networks, 5211, 5221, 5231, INET 4011, 4021, 4041, 4165, EE 5505** (networking). Sockets, TCP and UDP, HTTP, DNS, IP, routing. *Why NO:* the fundamentals are everyday knowledge and off the path. *Counterpoint:* latency and throughput reasoning in system design draws on it. *Ask:* does any later course assume it?
- **CSCI 5143 · Real-Time and Embedded Systems** (3, needs CSCI 4061). Microcontroller C, interrupts, scheduling, low-power wireless. *Why NO:* off the AI and systems path. *Ask:* does edge or embedded ML matter?
- **CSCI 4271W · Secure Software Systems** (4, writing intensive, needs 3081W). Threat modeling and vulnerabilities from design to OS. Ran every Fall and Spring, B+ average. *Why NO:* chosen against after first looking interesting. *Ask:* is it standard for a software engineer?
- **CSCI 4950 · Senior Software Project** (3, A-F). A team builds, ships and maintains software for users. *Why NO:* replaced by 4970W. *Ask:* which is easier to get into, and can both count?
- **CSCI 4011, 5106, 5161** (theory chain). Formal languages, programming languages, compilers. *Why NO:* off the path. *Ask:* does grad school expect any of it?
- **CSCI 5204 · Advanced Computer Architecture.** Instruction sets, microarchitecture, memory. Needs CSCI 4203 first. *Why NO:* hardware, not software system design.
- **EE 5239 · Nonlinear Optimization for ML and AI** (3). Convex analysis, Lagrange multipliers, duality, LLM training examples. Needs EE 3025, MATH 2373 and 2374, or consent. *Why NO:* the prerequisites. *Ask:* would the EE department consent given CSCI 2033, 5304 and 5521?
- **IE 3013 · Optimization for Machine Learning** (4). Needs MATH 2263 or 2374. *Why NO:* no Calc III.
- **IE 3011 · Optimization Models and Methods** (4). Linear, integer, network and nonlinear optimization. *Why NO:* registration blocked it on the math prerequisite ("MATH 2374, MATH 2142 or equivalent"), decided 2026-11-19.
- **IE 5533 · Operations Research for Data Science** (3). LP and integer programming in AMPL, stochastic models, R. You qualify and the course average is A-. *Why NO:* dropped from every semester after CSCI 5561 took the open credits, decided 2026-11-19.
- **CSCI 5707** duplicates CSCI 4707 (credit exclusion). **EE 5371** needs CSCI 4203 and consent. **INET 4061, 4062, 4711** overlap CSCI 4521 and 5521. **MATH 5465** and **STAT 4051, 4052** need prerequisites outside the plan.
