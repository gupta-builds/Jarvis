# Fall'27
## Purpose
==Fall'27 closes **CSCI 3081W**, starts the year-long entrepreneurship course, and stacks Cloud, Deep Learning and NLP.== The advisor questions live in [[Advisor Meeting]], the ledger in [[APAS]], the previous term in [[Spring'27 Syllabus]]. The Fall 2027 schedule was unpublished, so every offering is a guess from Fall 2026 and earlier. Nothing here is registered or earned.
## Classes
==The plan is **20 credits across six courses**, the CSE limit without a petition, and it stays above 17.== It assumes Spring'27 ends at 114 credits, that **CSCI 5561** cleared its waitlist, and that earlier courses are passed.
- **CSCI 3081W · Program Design and Development** (4). *Role:* required CS core, writing intensive. A team project: design, testing, refactoring, version control, technical writing. Prerequisites CSCI 2021 and 2041 are done. *Risk:* section and language (Spring'27 had C++ and Java).
- **MGMT 4171W · Entrepreneurship in Action I** (4). *Role:* minor elective, writing intensive. The fall half of a year-long startup launch, with up to $15,000. *Needs:* application and consent. It closed full in Fall 2026.
- **CSCI 4821 · GenAI for Software Engineering** (3). *Role:* counts toward the last technical-elective credit. *Needs:* instructor consent to run beside 3081W. It was full in Fall 2026 and Spring 2027.
- **CSCI 5144 · Cloud Computing** (3). *Role:* extra, and the **closest course to system design**: cloud service models, datacenter resource management and scheduling, design patterns and APIs, storage durability and consistency. Not on the audit's list, so the advisor must add it.
- **CSCI 5527 · Deep Learning** (3). *Role:* extra. Backprop, autodiff, CNNs, RNNs, generative nets, deep RL. Needs CSCI 5521. *Risk:* no Fall section in 2024 to 2026, and undergraduates need the instructor's permission.
- **CSCI 5541 · Natural Language Processing** (3). *Role:* extra. Text classification, language representations, LLMs, ethics. Needs CSCI 5521. Ran Fall and Spring. Moved here from Spring'28 because Computer Vision moved to Spring'27.
### Left Out On Purpose
- **CSCI 5561 Computer Vision** moved to Spring'27 (waitlist).
- **CSCI 5994** skips this term to stay under the 20-credit cap. The **Literature** course waits for Spring'28.
### Collisions And Fallbacks
- *4821 versus 5144:* in Fall 2026 both met TuTh 11:15. If Fall'27 repeats that, **CSCI 5144 wins** because it is the system-design course.
- *No Fall section of CSCI 5527:* **CSCI 5561 Computer Vision** takes that slot if it was not taken in Spring'27, otherwise the term drops to 17 and **CSCI 5994** adds 1 to 2 credits. Deep Learning then moves to Spring'28.
- *No section of CSCI 5144, or a clash:* **CSCI 5451** Parallel Computing or **CSCI 4203** Computer Architecture, both MAYBE.
- *CSCI 4821 refused:* the term drops to 17, so add **CSCI 5994** for 1 to 2 credits.
- *No seat in MGMT 4171W:* **MGMT 4173** and **MGMT 4008** (2 credits each, second half). The minor is then 1 credit short; fill it in Spring'28 with MGMT 4085, MOT 4001 or MGMT 4175W.
- *If the CSCI 5561 waitlist failed in Spring'27:* Computer Vision comes back here and NLP goes to Spring'28, because both together make 23 credits.
