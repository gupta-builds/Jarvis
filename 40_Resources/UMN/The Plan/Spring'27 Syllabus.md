## Spring'27 Classes
==The schedule is **20 credits across eight courses**, the CSE limit without a petition.== Tuition is flat from 13 credits. An undergraduate F-1 student needs 12 credits with at least 9 in person; only **CSCI 5521** is online.
- **CSCI 5561 · Computer Vision** (3). *Role:* technical elective, pulled forward from Fall'27. Catalog text is classical vision (edges, filtering, segmentation, tracking, stereo, shape recovery). *Status:* **waitlist**. *Needs:* CSCI 4521 or 5521 (4521 is in progress).
- **CSCI 5512 · Artificial Intelligence II** (3). *Role:* technical elective. Probability, utility and decisions under uncertainty.
- **CSCI 5521 · Machine Learning Fundamentals** (3, live online). *Role:* technical elective. The theory behind ML; the gateway to **CSCI 5527** and **5541**.
- **CSCI 4707 · Practice of Database Systems** (3). *Role:* technical elective. "In the Age of AI": SQL, query plans, normalization, then vector databases, RAG and text-to-SQL.
- **IBUS 3055 · AI & Tech Entrepreneurship** (4). *Role:* minor elective. Second half of the term. **Includes a May 14 to 27 trip to Germany and Switzerland.** *Needs:* consent from carlsonminors@umn.edu, the trip cost, an ISSS check.
- **MOT 4002 · Technically Speaking Leadership Lecture Series** (1). *Role:* minor elective. A-F only.
- **CSCI 5994 · Directed Research** (2 intended). *Role:* general elective if S/N. Section 002 with **Ali Anwar**. *Needs:* his consent; registration defaults to 1 credit.
- **CSCI 3901 · Introduction to Undergraduate Research in CS** (1). *Role:* general elective, S/N only. Research cycle, reading literature, writing a proposal, research programs such as URGO, McNair and DREU. *Status:* enroll, attend the first lecture, then keep or drop.

### If The Waitlist Does Not Clear
- The term stands at 17 credits until it does, which is still above the F-1 minimum.
