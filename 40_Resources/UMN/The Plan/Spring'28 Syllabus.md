# Spring'28
## Purpose
==Spring'28 is the final semester: it carries the **Literature** course, the minor's last course, database internals, a project course, and one slot still to choose.== The advisor questions live in [[Advisor Meeting]], the ledger in [[APAS]]. The Spring 2028 schedule was unpublished, so everything is placed from past Spring terms.
## Classes
==The plan is **15 credits across five courses plus one open slot**, which brings the term to 18 or 19.== It must end above 17 and stays under the 20-credit cap. It assumes Fall'27 ends at 134 credits.
- **A Literature course** (3, to choose). *Role:* the last required liberal education course. Spring'27 candidates were ENGL 1051 Progress & Madness: Literature, Science & Technology, ENGL 1701 Modern Fiction and ENGL 1011 Laughter and Literature.
- **MGMT 4172 · Entrepreneurship in Action II** (4). *Role:* minor elective. Launches the business started in MGMT 4171W. *Tentative:* decided later. Needs MGMT 4171W.
- **CSCI 5708 · Architecture and Implementation of DBMS** (3). *Role:* extra. Storage, indexing, query optimization, transactions, concurrency, recovery. Runs only in Spring.
- **CSCI 4970W · Advanced Project Laboratory** (3). *Role:* extra, writing intensive. A team project arranged with faculty, ending in a built artifact. Repeatable up to 9 credits. Needs CSCI 4061 and consent. MAYBE leaning yes.
- **CSCI 5994 · Directed Research** (2). *Role:* general elective if S/N. The research thread from Spring'27.
- **Open slot** (3 to 4). *Role:* extra. Not chosen yet.
### The Open Slot
==One MAYBE course fills it; choose when the Spring 2028 schedule publishes.== None of these is confirmed for Spring'28.
- **STAT 3301 Regression and Statistical Computing** (4). Ran Fall and Spring, B+ average. The statistics course the record argues for (STAT 3021 was a B), but late in the plan.
- **CSCI 5271 Introduction to Computer Security** (3). Ran Fall and Spring. Needs CSCI 4061.
- **CSCI 5451 Parallel Computing** (3). Ran Fall and Spring.
- **CSCI 4203 Computer Architecture** (4). Ran Fall and Spring. Opens EE 5351 GPU programming.
- **CSCI 5421 Advanced Algorithms** (3). Plays to the A in CSCI 4041.
- **CSCI 5523 Data Mining** (3). Applied classical ML.
*If Deep Learning missed Fall'27:* **CSCI 5527** takes the open slot if it runs in Spring 2028 (it ran Spring 2025 and 2026). It needs the instructor's permission.
*If the term runs hot:* cut CSCI 5994 and CSCI 4970W first.

## Standing On Graduation
Expected graduation stays Spring 2028. Literature is the only degree requirement left in the last term, so it must not slip. Spring'28 enrollment must still meet the F-1 minimum of 12 credits; the plan is 18 or 19. Before the term, check the graduation application deadline and run a final APAS.
> [!QUESTION] Is 152 credits worth it?
> About 32 credits beyond 120 buy no degree progress. They buy databases, vision, deep learning, NLP, cloud and project work. Tuition is flat from 13 credits to the 20-credit cap, so the cost is time, not money.
