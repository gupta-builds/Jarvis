## What Is Established About CPT
==The regulation did not change; the guidance did; a court has not touched the guidance; and both sides leave schools one opening, a training that is truly required of everyone in a program.==
### Settled
1. **The regulation is unchanged.** 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i), July 1, 2026 version, still says "an integral part of an established curriculum" and lists "required internship or practicum" ([[CPT Program]]).
2. **The guidance is in force.** BCM 2608-01 (Aug 12) and BCM 2608-02 (Aug 24) tell DSOs to authorize CPT only when the training is required of all students in the degree, and an optional elective fails. Both say they are not rules ([[CPT Broadcast Messages]]).
3. **The school defines the requirement.** BCM 2608-01 says "the school, not the U.S. government, defines what is and is not required to complete a degree."
4. **The government's own brief leaves the opening.** Its October 28 filing says schools can keep authorizing CPT "provided the training is truly integral to their program of study" and sends other training to OPT ([[CPT Lawsuit]]).
5. **UMN has paused.** The ISSS CPT page and the CSE co-op international page both still say so on November 5.
### Not Settled
1. Whether the judge rules on November 6 or later, and how wide any relief reaches. UMN belongs to the AAU, one of four plaintiffs, but the relief asked for covers members and their students, and the judge decides the scope.
2. Whether and how fast ISSS restarts after a ruling. The advisor said on October 23 she had "genuinely no idea."
3. Whether any UMN program has, or could create, a training required of every student.
4. How ISSS treats CPT already on an I-20 from before August.
### The One Structural Path
BCM 2608-02 says all students, foreign and American, must do the training if the curriculum makes it integral, and that the employer needs a cooperative agreement with the school. A CS elective, and the optional CSE co-op, fail that test as written. A required practicum or co-op for every student in a program would pass it. That is a curriculum decision by the department and college, so ISSS cannot make it alone, and I need to find out who can.
### What This Means For Summer 2027
I plan as if CPT does not return before the March 2027 decision point and treat a ruling as upside ([[Junior Year Internship Decision]]). A stay would also not make UMN restart on its own, as [[SEVP and DSOs]] explains.
