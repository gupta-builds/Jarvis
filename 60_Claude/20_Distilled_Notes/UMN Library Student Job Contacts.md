# UMN Twin Cities Library Job Contacts (East Bank / West Bank)

Research goal: quiet, low-traffic library buildings at UMN-TC where a student worker could realistically do homework/coding during shifts. Walter Library, Bruininks Hall, and Tate Hall excluded per request (too popular/crowded, or not actually libraries — see note below).

**Method:** Fetched each official `lib.umn.edu` / `law.umn.edu` page directly, decoded the Cloudflare-obfuscated email links in the raw HTML (they render as "[email protected]" to normal viewers/most scrapers), and cross-checked against the library's own staff directory. Every email below came from an official .edu page — none are guessed or pattern-generated. Source URL is listed for each so you can re-verify before emailing.

## Important correction

**Bruininks Hall** and **Tate Hall** are **not libraries**:
- Bruininks Hall = classrooms + One Stop Student Services + student lounge/café (formerly "Science Teaching and Student Services" building).
- Tate Hall (John T. Tate Hall) = home of the School of Physics & Astronomy. It contains a small non-public rare-books physics collection, not a staffed public library with student-worker jobs.

So there's nothing to exclude there — they were never candidates. The real UMN-TC library system (outside Walter) is smaller and quieter than you'd think, which works in your favor.

## Start here: central Libraries HR (covers every branch below except Law)

All the branches under "University of Minnesota Libraries" (Wilson, Andersen, Architecture, Music, Health Sciences, Wangensteen — and Walter) share **one HR office**. This is your single best first email for a shift-based/quiet-desk student job across any of these buildings.

- **Libraries HR (student jobs):** `tlib-hr@umn.edu`
  Source: https://www.lib.umn.edu/about/student-jobs — page text: *"With questions, please email the Libraries HR team at tlib-hr@umn.edu."*
- Official posting board: https://hr.umn.edu/applicant-center/student-jobs/find-student-job — filter by Academic & Administrative Unit → **"Libraries, University."**
- General central admin office is physically inside Wilson Library: 499 Wilson Library, 309 19th Ave S, Minneapolis, MN 55455. Desk phone (all-branch general line): 612-624-3321.

## Suggested outreach order
1. `tlib-hr@umn.edu` — one email, mention you're specifically interested in a quiet branch (Andersen, Architecture, Music, or Wangensteen) and ask if they're hiring or take standing applications.
2. In parallel, email the **named contact** at the specific branch (e.g. Kris Kiesling at Andersen, Deborah Ultan at Architecture) directly — branch supervisors sometimes hire independently of the central posting cycle, and a name in the "To:" field gets read before an unaddressed one.
3. For Law specifically, email Tyler Hall (`hall0550@umn.edu`) rather than the general desk.
4. Check https://hr.umn.edu/applicant-center/student-jobs/find-student-job periodically — Libraries postings show up there even when a branch doesn't reply to cold email.

## Draft outreach email

One template, reused per branch — swap the bracketed bits (name/branch/greeting). Keep it short; library staff get a lot of these.

> **Subject:** Student worker interest - [Library Name] (quiet desk / shelving / circulation)
>
Dear [Contact Name],

My name is Anant Gupta. I’m a junior at the University of Minnesota studying Computer Science with an Entrepreneurship minor.

I’m reaching out because I looked through the student job postings in detail but couldn’t find any current openings for [Library Name]. Rather than assume there isn’t a need, I thought I’d reach out directly and ask.

I’m interested in pretty much any student role you regularly hire for - circulation, shelving, stacks, service desk, helping patrons, basic library operations, or whatever else needs an extra set of hands. I’m not particularly tied to one type of work. If there’s a role where I can be useful and dependable, I’d be interested in it. 

One thing that makes library work especially appealing to me is the nature of the work. I’m taking 22 credits this semester, so I’m trying to find a job where I can be productive during the quieter parts of a shift when there isn’t anything that needs to be done immediately. I’m completely comfortable doing the work first - reshelving, desk coverage, helping people, closing tasks, or anything else that comes up and then using genuinely quiet time to keep up with coursework. If there’s always something else that needs doing, I’m happy to do that instead.

I’ve attached my resume so you have some background on me. If there is an opening that looks like a fit, I’d be happy to submit the official application and write a more detailed cover letter as well. If you’re not the right person to ask, I’d really appreciate it if you could point me toward whoever handles student hiring for the library. Even a quick “nothing available right now” would be helpful, since I’m reaching out to a few libraries across campus and trying to keep track of where there may be opportunities.

If it would be easier to talk for a few minutes, you can also book a time directly on my calendar here: https://cal.com/gupta-builds/fall-26

Thanks for taking a look at this. I’d be glad to hear either way.

Best,
Anant


**Notes on using it:**
- For Wilson, send this to `tlib-hr@umn.edu` with "Libraries HR Team" as the greeting since there's no named contact.
- For Andersen/Architecture/Music/Health Sciences/Wangensteen, address it to the named contact above and CC the department fallback email so it doesn't get missed if they're out.
- For Law, send to Tyler Hall and mention you understand the Law Library hires separately from the central University Libraries system.
- Keep the "22 credits / want to stay productive during downtime" framing in the second paragraph — it's honest and reads as self-aware rather than as an ask for slack, but don't lead the email with it; lead with the direct job ask.
